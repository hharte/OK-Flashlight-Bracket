# Installation

Use the included STL mesh files (14mm or 18mm) to print the bracket using a 3D printer.  I suggest using PETG filament for strength.  Use an M3x20 cap head bolt and M3 nut to attach the bracket to the rear of the Speed-Wrap.

Use two small zip ties to secure the flashlight to the top of the bracket.

# Cleaning, Lubricating, Rebuilding

The bearings are ⅜” OD, ⅛” ID, 5/32” Width.  These are some of the available bearing replacements on Amazon:

[https://www.amazon.com/HiPicco-R2-2RS-Bearings-Bearing-Pre-Lubricated/dp/B0C74WTY1K](https://www.amazon.com/HiPicco-R2-2RS-Bearings-Bearing-Pre-Lubricated/dp/B0C74WTY1K)

[https://www.amazon.com/uxcell-R2-2RS-Groove-Bearing-Bearings/dp/B0824WN2RB](https://www.amazon.com/uxcell-R2-2RS-Groove-Bearing-Bearings/dp/B0824WN2RB)

[https://www.amazon.com/uxcell-8-inchx3-8-inchx5-Shielded-Bearings/dp/B0824VSF74](https://www.amazon.com/uxcell-8-inchx3-8-inchx5-Shielded-Bearings/dp/B0824VSF74)

***<span style="text-decoration:underline;">Note:</span>***

When cleaning and lubricating the gears and bearings, it is very important to get the ring gear aligned properly.  Don’t press the bearing all the way into the recess in the case.  Instead, press the bearing in slightly, just enough to hold the ring gear in place while you assemble the tool.  While tightening the case, the bearing will be pushed into the recess just enough to maintain proper contact with the pinion gear.  If the tool is noisy, or doesn’t return quickly when the handle is released, the gear alignment is most likely incorrect.  Take it apart again, and assemble as described above.


## Lubrication of gears

Use a small amount of a light grease such as [Permatex White Lithium Grease](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B000HBNV58).
